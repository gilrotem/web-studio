# Web design craft bar, November 2026

State-of-the-craft research on what premium web design and its execution looked like in
October-November 2026. It is dated evidence, not a standing rulebook.

## What is here

- `report.md` - the synthesis, "Directed restraint sets the 2026 craft bar". It ends with
  "Implications for the studio" (version pins, performance and motion gates, accessibility and
  legal gates, Hebrew and RTL rules, WordPress delivery rules, method rules) and "What could not
  be verified".
- `notes/` - the five research notes the report was synthesised from:
  - `design_bar.md` - what award-tier sites look like and what juries reward.
  - `execution_stack.md` - the front-end stack and delivery as a hand-coded WordPress theme.
  - `ai_design_method.md` - how practitioners produce top-tier design with coding agents.
  - `motion_material.md` - motion, transitions and visual material.
  - `hebrew_rtl_israel.md` - Hebrew typography, RTL craft and Israeli web standards and law.

## Date and how it was made

Only what the files themselves state:

- Research date: 9 November 2026. The report says it was synthesised from the five notes
  gathered on that date, and that where a claim is an inference rather than something a source
  states, the text says so.
- The notes say they rest on web search plus direct fetches of primary pages on that date; the
  Hebrew note adds raw-HTML reads of real Israeli sites and a query of the Fontsource API.
- Each note is organised by key question, and each question has a Takeaway, Cited Findings,
  Inferences and Gaps. Items a source could not confirm are marked unverified or listed under
  Gaps; items older than 2025 are marked background.
- Every claim carries its source link inline.

## Scope to keep in mind

The research was run against one target profile: a premium, Hebrew-only, RTL
professional-services site delivered as a hand-coded WordPress theme on Hostinger shared hosting
(LiteSpeed Cache, PHP 8.3). Where a regulated profession needed a concrete case, the worked
example is a real-estate appraiser. Findings about award juries, the stack, motion and agent
method are general; the Israeli law, Hebrew type and hosting findings are specific to that
profile.

The material was carried into this kit in full. Wording that referred to the original engagement
was generalised; no source, number, date or finding was removed.

## Which kit skills lean on it

At packaging time these skills cite this research (they refer to it as the `craft-research`
skill):

- `motion-language` - the `motion_material` note.
- `hebrew-rtl-israel` - the `hebrew_rtl_israel` note.
- `wp-theme` - the `execution_stack` note.
- `studio-research` - the full report and its five notes, for depth on the current state of the
  craft.

## Staleness

Version numbers, Baseline statuses, prices, legal thresholds and award results here are true as
of 9 November 2026 and will drift. Treat this folder as evidence with a date on it. When a skill
needs a number from it and the date is old, re-run the research and add a new dated folder next
to this one rather than editing these files.
