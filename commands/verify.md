Follow the `visual-verify` skill.

Target: $ARGUMENTS

Pick the mode, and say which one you are running before you start:
- **Every design job (a site, a redesign, a page) → REFINE.** Shoot it (`scripts/verify.mjs shoot`), look at it whole, name the single weakest thing, fix it at the root, shoot again. Repeat. The loop is the builder's own and ends when the piece is ready for the jury, not when a checklist clears.
- **A source reproduced exactly → FIDELITY.** Screenshot against the source, build the deviation table, treat every deviation not written in Guardrails as a defect.

Check RTL separately in both. After three passes with no improvement, hand the piece to the jury instead of grinding.
